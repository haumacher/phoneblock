# PhoneBlock – Data Exchange Agreement

## Parties

This agreement is made between:

- **[Name and address of the PhoneBlock operator]**, operator of the PhoneBlock service at phoneblock.net ("**PhoneBlock**"), and
- **[Legal name and address of the Nize operator]**, operator of the Nize caller-ID app ("**Nize**"),

each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**".

The Parties exchange spam reports made by their own users, so that both can better protect those users from unwanted calls. Neither Party pays the other for the data; each Party's data is the consideration for the other's.

## 1. Data exchanged

1.1 Each Party provides the other, through an authenticated API, with the spam reports its own users made directly in its service (the "**Reports**"). A Report consists of the phone number, the report time, a report category, a spam score, a pseudonymous reporter ID and, where given, the user's comment.

1.2 Each Party provides only Reports from its own users. It does not pass on data it received from the other Party or from any third party.

1.3 Each Party decides alone how it uses received Reports in its own rating, and may stop providing or using them at any time.

## 2. Permitted use

2.1 Each Party grants the other a non-exclusive, royalty-free, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to use the Reports it provides, including for commercial purposes, **solely to operate the receiving Party's own call-protection and caller-identification app and service** (the "**Permitted Use**").

2.2 The Permitted Use includes storing the Reports, combining them with the receiving Party's own data into aggregated ratings (for example a number's spam rating or a block list), and showing those ratings and the comments on a number within the receiving Party's own apps and website.

2.3 For the Reports provided under this agreement, this licence replaces the Creative Commons BY-NC-SA licence under which PhoneBlock publishes its data. That public licence remains unchanged for all other data and users.

## 3. No passing on

3.1: no passing on, selling or sublicensing of reports as data records, individually or in bulk, and no partner feeds, data exchanges or bulk downloads. Reporter IDs are never disclosed.

3.2 showing a report's comment text and date together with the number it concerns is allowed within the party's own service: number pages, apps and a per-number lookup API.

3.3 Aggregated ratings into which Reports are merged with other data may be offered as part of the receiving Party's own service, including block lists and lookup or download APIs for its users and their devices. They must not be supplied to other caller-ID or spam-data providers, or to data brokers, under a data exchange or sale.

3.4 Excepted from 3.1 are service providers that process the data solely on behalf of and under instruction of the receiving Party (for example hosting), provided they are bound to this agreement's restrictions.

## 4. Data protection

4.1 Each Party is an independent controller for the Reports it holds and complies with the applicable data protection law, in particular the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

4.2 Reporter IDs are pseudonymous. The receiving Party must not try to identify the reporting users or to link Reports to other data about them.

4.3 If a Party has to delete or correct a Report it provided (for example after a data subject's request), the other Party deletes or corrects its stored copy, including any comment text, within 30 days of being notified.

## 5. No fees, no warranty

5.1 The data exchange is free of charge in both directions.

5.2 The Reports are user opinions and are provided "as is", without any warranty of accuracy, completeness or availability.

5.3 Each Party's liability under this agreement is limited to intent and gross negligence, as far as legally permitted.

5.4 Each Party may name the other as a source of spam reports, with a link to its service, in its app, on its website and in its communications, for example "Spam reports partly provided by PhoneBlock (phoneblock.net)". The Parties welcome such mentions. Use of the other Party's logo, and any statement suggesting an endorsement beyond this data exchange, needs prior consent in text form.

## 6. Term and termination

6.1 This agreement starts on signature by both Parties and runs for an indefinite period.

6.2 Either Party may terminate it at any time with 30 days' notice in text form (e-mail suffices). Either Party may terminate it without notice if the other Party materially breaches clauses 2, 3 or 4.

6.3 On termination, each Party stops receiving and importing new Reports and deletes the individual Reports it stored, including comment texts, within 30 days, also at its service providers. Contributions already merged into aggregated ratings need not be removed; they may remain until they expire in the ordinary course of the receiving Party's rating.

## 7. Final provisions

7.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of [Germany]. Place of jurisdiction is [the registered seat of PhoneBlock's operator].

7.2 Changes and additions require text form.

7.3 If a provision is invalid, the remaining provisions stay in force.
