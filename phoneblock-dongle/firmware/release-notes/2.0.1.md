# PhoneBlock Dongle 2.0.1

*unreleased*

## Added

- **ESP32-S3 Super Mini.** The S3 firmware now also runs on the ESP32-S3
  Super Mini with 16 MB flash (N16R8); the firmware no longer drives the
  T-Dongle-S3 display that board does not have (#730).
- **Choose your board's LED.** System ▸ Board selects a known board or sets
  the status LED (plain LED, APA102 or WS2812, on any free pin) and the
  display. Without a choice the dongle uses its guess, as before.
- **Colour on RGB LEDs.** Besides the blink pattern, an RGB LED shows the
  state in colour: green ready, red problem, white setup missing, amber
  connecting, blue WPS pairing.

## Fixed

- A SIP registrar that sends a very long nonce (more than about 160
  characters) no longer fails the login: the dongle cut the digest input
  off at 256 characters and sent a wrong response. The Fritz!Box is not
  affected (its nonces are 16 characters) (#726).
- Status mails reach Proton, t-online and other mail servers with a large
  certificate chain again; since 2.0.0 they failed with "TLS handshake
  failed: -0x7080". SIP over TLS is protected the same way (#734).
- The Fritz!Box two-factor hint during the automatic setup reads as one
  sentence again, without the stray period and with "or" in your language
  (#739).
