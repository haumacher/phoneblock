# PhoneBlock Dongle 2.0.1

*unreleased*

## Added

- **ESP32-S3 Super Mini.** The S3 firmware now also runs on the ESP32-S3
  Super Mini with 16 MB flash (N16R8); the firmware no longer drives the
  T-Dongle-S3 display that board does not have (#730).
- **Choose your board's LED.** System ▸ Board selects a known board or sets
  the status LED (plain LED, APA102 or WS2812, on any free pin) and the
  display. Without a choice the dongle uses its guess, as before.
- **Colour on RGB LEDs.** Besides the blink pattern, an RGB LED shows the
  state in colour: green ready, red problem, white setup missing, amber
  connecting, blue WPS pairing.
