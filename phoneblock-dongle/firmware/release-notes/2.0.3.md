# PhoneBlock Dongle 2.0.3

*unreleased*

- **Display on the LilyGO T-Display V1.1 (#708).** The classic ESP32
  firmware can now drive the 240x135 screen of this board: the logo with
  the spam badge, and the caller during a call, as on the T-Dongle-S3.
  Select "LilyGO T-Display V1.1" under System ▸ Board (or tick "Display
  einschalten"); the display is off by default on the ESP32. The text is
  drawn at twice the size, and a long caller name continues on a second
  line. The backlight goes off a minute after boot or after a call ends
  instead of dimming. Not offered on the EGBO dongle (ESP32-PICO-D4), whose
  flash sits on a display pin.
- **The caller stays on the display after the call (#708).** On the
  display (T-Dongle-S3 and T-Display) the caller is no longer replaced by
  the logo when the call ends, but stays until the display dims, a minute
  after the call ended. So you can still read a short call, for example a
  fax or a call answered on another phone.
