# PhoneBlock Dongle 1.7.0

*unreleased*

## Added

- **Act on calls straight from the call list.** "⚠ Spam" reports a call that
  got through to PhoneBlock; the number is blocked from the next call on.
  "＋ Kontakt" writes an unnamed caller into a Fritz!Box phonebook — for a
  blocked caller this also puts the number on your whitelist. Without a
  Fritz!Box, "✓ Whitelist" unblocks a wrongly blocked number. Each row shows
  the caller's current state, and offers only the actions that change it.

- A number on your own whitelist is no longer blocked by a caller-name
  pattern, and calls blocked by a pattern are reported to PhoneBlock like
  other blocked spam calls.

- **Choose which phonebook new contacts go to.** Asked once, changeable under
  "Telefonbuch für neue Kontakte". Phonebooks that synchronise with an online
  address book (CardDAV, Google) are shown but cannot be selected: the
  Fritz!Box rejects writes to them. Needs a dongle set up with the Fritz!Box
  wizard.

- **ESP32-C3 boards (e.g. "C3 Super Mini").** The firmware now also ships for
  the ESP32-C3, installable from the browser and updated over the air like the
  ESP32 dongle.

- **LilyGO T-Dongle-S3 (ESP32-S3).** A ready-made stick with a USB-A plug
  that goes straight into the Fritz!Box — no soldering, no adapter.
  Installable from the browser and updated over the air; the RGB LED shows
  the status (#602), the display shows the PhoneBlock logo (#606) and can
  be turned upside down under "System" (#608).

- **Chip temperature on the System card** for boards whose chip has a
  sensor (ESP32-C3); the classic ESP32 dongle has none.

## Fixed

- Many requests in a row (e.g. the first call-barring sync with many
  numbers) no longer run the dongle out of memory: closed connections now
  release their memory after 2 seconds instead of 2 minutes (#601).

- Saving a setting unrelated to SIP (LED, display, mail, …) no longer
  re-registers the dongle with the Fritz!Box (#611).

- About 8 KB more free memory in normal operation: Wi-Fi setup from the
  browser installer (USB) is available for 10 minutes after plugging in,
  then the service ends if the dongle is online (#599).

- A firmware or announcement upload no longer aborts on a short stall in
  the transfer; if it does fail, the log now says why (#597).

- Test calls (e.g. **622) are listed as "Test" and no longer counted as
  spam; anonymous and internal calls show "unbekannt" instead of "legitim".

- A rating comment containing a quotation mark would have produced a malformed
  request. Comments are now escaped correctly.

- If downloading the announcement fails after a restart, the dongle retries
  within minutes instead of a day later. A spam call answered without an
  announcement now leaves a warning in the log.

- The storage no longer fills up with the warning "SPIFFS_gc failed" while
  plenty of space is shown free (#529, #530, #542). The announcement, mail
  texts and your own recording now come first; the community blocklist kept on
  the dongle takes only the space that remains. While your own recording is
  active, the localized announcement is not stored.
